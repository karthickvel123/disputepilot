## 1. Engagement & Pilot Scope

1.1 **Purpose**: These Pilot Terms of Service ("Agreement") govern the evaluation and pilot operation of the DisputePilot platform ("Platform") by the participating merchant ("Merchant").

1.2 **Pilot Limitations**:
- The initial pilot is conducted using **Razorpay TEST API keys** (`rzp_test_*`) or a tightly scoped single-merchant live integration.
- The Platform functions strictly as a decision-support and workflow defense automation tool.
- **The Platform does NOT guarantee chargeback win rates, fund recovery, or reversal of bank debits.** Final adjudication of all payment disputes rests exclusively with the acquiring bank, the issuing bank, and the relevant card payment network (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex).

## 2. Responsibilities & Operational Invariants

2.1 **Human-in-the-Loop Requirement**:
- All final contest submissions (`PATCH /v1/disputes/:id/contest`) and chargeback concessions (`POST /v1/disputes/:id/accept`) **strictly require** explicit human authorization via a cryptographically signed, single-use `ApprovalToken`.
- DisputePilot **never** autonomously transfers funds, waives dispute rights, or submits unreviewed evidence to financial networks without merchant operator sign-off.

2.2 **Accuracy of Evidence**:
- The Merchant is solely responsible for the authenticity, legibility, and legal validity of evidence uploaded to the Platform (e.g., invoices, courier delivery receipts, customer communication logs).
- Fabricated, forged, or altered evidence is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate termination of the pilot and potential reporting under payment aggregator terms.

2.3 **Credential Security**:
- The Merchant must safeguard Razorpay API Keys (`RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`), Webhook Secrets (`RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), Session Secrets (`SESSION_SECRET`), and Gemini API Keys (`GEMINI_API_KEY`).
- Operator access is strictly role-based (Admin, Reviewer, Auditor) and tied to individual credentials. Shared generic passwords in production are strictly prohibited.
