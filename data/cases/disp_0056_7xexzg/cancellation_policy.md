# Apex Retail Solutions Private Limited — Terms of Sale, Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Document Reference:** TOS-POL-2024-V2  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Public Policy URL:** https://apexsolutions.example.in/terms-and-policies  

### 1. Order Cancellation Policy
Customers may cancel orders within 2 hours of payment or before the consignment is marked as 'Dispatched'. Once dispatched with our logistics partners, transit cancellation cannot be executed.

### 2. Returns and Defect Reporting
- Return requests must be lodged within 7 calendar days of verified courier delivery.
- Goods must be returned in unblemished original packaging with intact security seals.
- Defective or variant discrepancies must include unboxing photographs submitted within 48 hours of receipt.

### 3. Subscription Cancellations
- Subscription renewals auto-debit on the anniversary date.
- Cancellation requests must be logged at least 48 hours prior to the next billing cycle.
- Continued usage or access to merchant software/services after billing constitutes acceptance of the billing cycle.

### 4. Dispute Escalation & Refund SLA
- Approved refunds are routed directly through Razorpay to the originating payment instrument within 5–7 banking days.
- A valid Acquirer Reference Number (ARN) will be provided upon processing.
